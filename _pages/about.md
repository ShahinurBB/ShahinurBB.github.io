I am an economist with 8 years of experience at Bangladesh Bank and a background in applied microeconomics, causal inference, and data science. I recently completed my M.A. in Public Economics at the National Graduate Institute for Policy Studies (GRIPS) in Tokyo under the Japan-IMF Scholarship (JISPA).

My research interests focus on **development economics**, and **causal policy evaluation**, exploring how technology, institutional mechanisms, and shocks alter societal belief systems and economic behavior.

I am currently co-authoring empirical research with Prof. Jun Goto at GRIPS examining how technological adaptations to natural disasters influence religiosity and belief systems. 

I am actively preparing for PhD admissions in Economics.
